## Design Context

### Users
RunViz is for regular runners who want extra training data without needing a coaching platform or a complicated lab tool. They use it to review their recent running history, spot patterns, and make better training-plan decisions with a clear sense of progress, load, and readiness.

### Brand Personality
Simple, clean, and clear with a premium feel. The interface should feel focused and trustworthy rather than flashy, giving runners confidence that the data is useful and easy to act on.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined performance dashboard. Typography should carry a lot of the premium feel through strong hierarchy, restrained detail, and polished readability. The product should support both light and dark modes and follow the system theme, even if individual features are still being brought into alignment.

### Design Principles
1. Put clarity before ornament so runners can scan metrics quickly.
2. Use typography and spacing to create hierarchy instead of piling on labels or visual effects.
3. Keep the interface calm and premium, with confident contrast and disciplined detail.
4. Make analytics feel approachable for everyday runners, not only data obsessives.
5. Build components so they can adapt cleanly to both light and dark presentation over time.
